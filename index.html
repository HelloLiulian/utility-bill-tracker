Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Bills and reminders are stored on your device, with optional iCloud backup. There is no login and no account to lose.

Can I scan a paper bill?

Yes. The planned flow is photo capture with on-device text recognition. Any field the scanner gets wrong can be corrected before saving.

Does it pay bills or connect to my bank?

No. It reminds you and tracks what you owe. It does not move money, store bank credentials, or connect to utility accounts.

What will it cost?

The founding price is $14.99 one-time. There is no subscription. The free tier covers up to 5 bills.

When will it be available?

It is still in development. People on the early access list will be the first to try it and will get the founding price.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, with optional iCloud backup. The validation page you are reading does not collect bill data — only the email you choose to submit.
